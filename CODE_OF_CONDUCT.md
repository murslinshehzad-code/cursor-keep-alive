# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a friendly, safe place for everyone who takes part.

## Our standards

- Be respectful and patient. Assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- No sharing of other people's private information.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and comments.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who break these rules.
To report a problem, open an issue that does not include private details, or use GitHub's
"Report content" feature on the comment. Reports are handled fairly and kept as private as possible.
